About Bedsplit

Bedsplit cuts 3D models that are too big for your printer into parts that fit, adds joints, and packages everything ready to slice.

What it does

  • Opens STL, OBJ and 3MF files.
  • Finds the fewest parts that fit your bed, trying different model angles and using empty space instead of cutting through it.
  • Adds pegs, dowel holes or sliding dovetails, and plans the build order.
  • Can hollow a big model into a shell with a fixed wall first, so parts print as rings with pegs built into the wall.
  • Exports print-ready STLs, a combined 3MF, printable pins and an assembly sheet.

Private by design

All processing happens in your browser. Your models are never uploaded to a server. See the privacy policy.

Free to use

Bedsplit is free. Ads, affiliate links and optional donations pay for it.

Try it on your model. Bedsplit runs in your browser, and your files never upload.
