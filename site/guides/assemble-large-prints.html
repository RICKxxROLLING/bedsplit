Gluing and finishing large multi-part prints

Joints line up the parts, and glue and filler make them look like one piece.

Dry-fit first

Put everything together without glue. Sand down any pegs that bind, and check the order using the assembly sheet in the zip. Parts are numbered bottom to top.

Glue

  • Medium CA glue (superglue) works for PLA, PETG and most other filaments. A spray of activator sets it in seconds.
  • Two-part epoxy is stronger and fills gaps, but it's slow. Use it on load-bearing seams.
  • Plastic welding means using a soldering iron and a strand of the same filament to melt a bead into the seam. It's very strong and easy to sand.

Hide the seams

  1. Fill gaps with glazing putty or a CA-and-baking-soda mix.
  2. Sand from 120 grit up to 400 grit.
  3. Spray filler primer, sand again, then paint.

Print orientation tips

Parts from the print_ready/ folder already sit on a flat cut face, which keeps the joint surfaces crisp and the seams tight. Keep wall count and infill the same on every part so shrinkage matches.
