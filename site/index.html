Model

Rotate 90°

Keeps a solid outer wall and removes the inside, so big models print as rings and panels instead of solid blocks. Joints shrink to fit inside the wall.

Printer

Mold

Builds a block with your model as a hollow inside, cut into parts that lock together on keys. Clamp the parts, pour silicone or epoxy into the funnel, then open the mold to get a cast of the model.

At the model’s widest section

Pours from the top (+Z). Turn the model with Rotate so the side where a funnel stub is easiest to trim faces up.

Joints

Cuts

Searches cut positions piece by piece, and optionally the model's angle, to reach the lowest part count. It spots empty space, so pieces that are already separate are never cut through air, and each part only needs to fit the bed in some orientation.

Parts

The zip holds each mold part as an STL turned with its parting face up (so the cavity prints as an open pit, no supports), a 3MF with every part, and a sheet with casting steps and how much resin to mix.

The zip holds one STL per part, a single 3MF with every part (press A in Bambu Studio to arrange across plates), printable pins or dovetail keys when needed, and an assembly sheet with the build order.
